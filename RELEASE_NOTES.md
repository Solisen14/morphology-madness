# Morphology Madness v1.0

**Release date:** November 3, 2026

This is the first publicly shared, classroom-ready version of Morphology Madness, a free browser-based vocabulary and word-invention resource.

### Included in v1.0

- **Word Wizard:** five randomized real-word challenges per game, with clues, word-part cards, hints, feedback, and results.
- **Meaning Madness:** five randomized imaginative definitions per game, with broad meaning-tag checking and explained, self-reviewed alternatives.
- **Word Laboratory:** an animated three-reel slot machine generating an invented word; students then write a definition and reflect on its meaning.
- **380 word-part entries** (70 prefixes/initial forms, 250 roots/bases, 60 suffixes/final forms), shared among all modes.
- **40 real-word challenges** and **30 creative prompts** spanning five subjects.
- **Apprentice / Inventor / Master** levels, with 3 / 6 / 10 choices per category in the card-building games.
- Mobile and Chromebook-friendly design, reduced-motion support, exit confirmation, and session-only summaries.
- No accounts, analytics, server-side student data, or multiplayer.

### Known limitations

- The game does not automatically verify creative definitions or linguistic correctness of arbitrary invented words.
- Word Wizard uses curated intended solutions; it does not accept all conceivable alternate answers.
- Words are joined from written cards without automatic spelling changes.
- Repetition is possible because subject-specific clue libraries are finite.
- No student work is stored beyond the current browser session.
