# Grade 10 Canadian History Vocabulary (Ontario CHC2D and CHC2P, revised 2026)

**Grade 10 History** is now a dedicated subject selection in the three existing Morphology Madness games. **History**, the original general bank, remains available. This curated vocabulary serves both CHC2D and CHC2P as a supporting literacy tool; it is not a replacement for either course or a Ministry-required glossary.

## Curriculum and date

Ontario revised **Canadian History since World War I** for the **2026–27 school year**, retaining separate **Academic (CHC2D)** and **Applied (CHC2P)** courses.

## Non-negotiable historical teaching considerations

The 2026 curriculum includes expanded learning about the experiences and contributions of Black Canadians; extreme political and social ideologies; the Holocaust and rising antisemitism; and the Holodomor famine in Ukraine. These subjects require rigorous, historically contextualized teaching using source evidence, testimonies, and appropriate resources.

- **Do not use fictional or invented words to teach or trivialize atrocities, identities, the Holocaust, the Holodomor, genocide, antisemitism, or human suffering.** Meaning Madness prompts focus on neutral historical inquiry, fictional tools, and cooperation instead.
- The real-word bank includes a small number of terms such as *antisemitism*, *genocide*, *persecution*, *internment*, and *discrimination* solely as accurate vocabulary for direct teaching and discussion. Their definitions must not substitute for careful historical study.
- Terms such as *Holocaust* and *Holodomor* are **not** forced into morphological slots. Teachers should explicitly teach names, historical evidence, and lived experiences without presenting artificial word divisions.
- For *antisemitism*, do not treat its historical root as indicating a prejudice against all speakers of Semitic languages. The term specifically means hostility, prejudice, or discrimination against **Jewish people**.
- Honour the specificity of First Nations, Métis, Inuit, Black Canadian, and other community experiences. Morphological analysis is one language-learning support, not a complete explanation of historical structures or harms.

## Editorial validation

1. All **97** real Word Wizard answers assemble exactly from **two or three meaningful word parts**, not an arbitrary syllable split. Some roots are historical bound forms, not standalone modern English words.
2. Canadian spelling is used where suitable (*labourer*, *mobilization*, *decolonization*). Variant spellings and grammatical processes require teacher explanation.
3. Grade 10 terms are chosen for usefulness to historical inquiry and for possible appearance in the course's thematic study, **not** as proof that students must memorize every word.
4. Creative prompts remain neutral and self-reviewed. The game does not automatically grade students' invented meanings. The student-facing site, settings and privacy behaviour are unchanged.
5. The shared History option and all existing Grade 9 subject choices are preserved.
