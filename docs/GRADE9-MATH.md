## Curriculum basis

The original challenge text is based on mathematical terminology used across the 2021 Grade 9 MTH1W curriculum, with broad strand tags for Number (B), Algebra (C), Data (D), Geometry and Measurement (E), and Financial Literacy (F). These tags do **not** claim full specific-expectation mapping or Ministry endorsement.

- Ontario Grade 9 Math curriculum: https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-mathematics/courses/mth1w
- EQAO Grade 9 assessment overview: https://www.eqao.com/the-assessments/grade-9-math/

This bank exercises morphologically approachable mathematical vocabulary and is **not** a complete EQAO vocabulary intervention. Phrases such as `at least`, `no more than`, or `closest estimate` need explicit contextual instruction; they cannot reliably be decoded using the game's prefix/base/suffix mechanic. Historical bound stems and ordinary word roots are identified as word parts, but arbitrary combinations are not automatically real words. The existing Word Wizard intended-answer rule and Meaning Madness semantic tags remain unchanged.

The general Mathematics and other subject banks are unchanged except that the three new affixes/endings enter the common card pool. Mixed mode may draw either Mathematics or Grade 9 Math rounds.

## Curated expansion (November 2026)

Added **40** new, original Word Wizard entries to the previous 30, for **70** Grade 9 Math real-word challenges. The additions deliberately include elementary prerequisite vocabulary. All **70** Grade 9 Math answers have at least **two** distinct, meaning-bearing components (prefix + base, base + suffix, or all three) and assemble exactly as spelled. No artificial breaks were introduced to pad the count. A few words use historically bound Latin/Greek forms; the explanatory text gives the relevant mathematical meaning.

### New additions by curriculum strand

**B**: equality, fractional, divisible, divisor, noninteger, nonnegative, overestimate, underestimate, miscalculation, recalculation.

**C**: algebraic, distributive, substitution, equivalent, rearrangement.

**D**: bivariate, univariate, predictable, misleading, representation, misrepresentation.

**E**: intersection, bisector, semicircle, diameter, equilateral, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, circumference, isometric, geometric.

**F**: taxable, nontaxable, investment, reinvestment, repayment, prepayment, overpayment, overcharge.

Original Math terms like *subtraction*, *intersection*, and *bisector* may appear in both the general Mathematics subject and Grade 9 Math, because they are helpful prerequisite vocabulary. The Grade 9 selection never contains duplicate answers within its own pool.

**Instructional boundary:** Important terms such as *slope* and *median* still do not necessarily split into two natural, relevant word parts, so they are not Word Wizard puzzles solely to increase the bank size.
