# Grade 9 Geography (Ontario CGC1W, 2024)

The **Grade 9 Geography** selection has been added alongside general Geography in the **School Subject** dropdown. It works with Word Wizard, Meaning Madness, and Word Laboratory without adding a new game or changing the existing layout.

## Official curriculum basis

Ontario Grade 9 *Exploring Canadian Geography*, de-streamed **CGC1W (2024)**. The words are original instructional examples informed by the curriculum, **not** a Ministry-issued glossary or an expectation-by-expectation claim of alignment.

## Editorial safeguards

- Every real Word Wizard answer is built from at least **two meaning-bearing word parts**, with prefixes/initial forms, bases/roots, and suffixes/final forms used where linguistically sound. The program verifies that joining the chosen parts produces the exact spelling.
- No arbitrary syllable divisions are used to inflate the list. Bound historical roots are marked accordingly, so students do not mistake them for freely usable modern words.
- Geography topics such as *watershed*, *floodplain*, *density*, and long multiword terms may need direct instruction. They are not forced into the game's three-slot mechanic.
- Some words also appear in general Geography, but the Grade 9 Geography pool has no duplicate target answers. Shared words and roots remain available to their original subjects.
- Meaning Madness uses broad semantic tags and student explanations rather than automatically judging whether an imaginary word is linguistically valid.
- Broad A–E strand tags identify the source of the vocabulary theme, not a claim that the word itself is required by a specific curriculum expectation.
- No student records, account, analytics, or submissions have been added.

## Representative words

**Inquiry and mapping:** geospatial, cartographer, cartography, topography, geolocation.

**Physical geography:** hydrosphere, lithosphere, meteorology, glaciology, erosion, deposition.

**Resources and industries:** renewable, nonrenewable, sustainability, deforestation, hydroelectric, agriculture.

**Changing populations:** immigration, demographic, bilingual, intercultural, resettlement.

**Liveable communities:** urbanization, residential, accessibility, affordability, walkability, liveability.
