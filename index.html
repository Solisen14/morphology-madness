What's in a word?

Every card carries a little piece of meaning.

pre-

Prefix

A meaningful part added before a base. pre- often means “before.”

struct

Base / Root

The central meaningful part. struct comes from a Latin root meaning “build.”

-ology

Suffix / Combining form

An ending that contributes meaning. -ology refers to a field of study.

Not every real word needs three pieces. Some scientific words use Greek or Latin combining forms, rather than ordinary English prefixes and suffixes. Spellings and meanings can change when parts combine. Creative words are inventions, not necessarily dictionary entries.
