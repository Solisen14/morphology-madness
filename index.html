What's in a word?

pre-

Prefix

A meaningful part added to the beginning of a word. re- often means “again.”

struct

Base / Root

The central meaningful part. struct comes from a Latin root meaning “build.”

-ion

Suffix

A meaningful part added to the end. -ion often forms a noun for an act or process.

Some cards are Greek or Latin combining forms. Not all words require three pieces, and spellings can change when parts join. This first prototype uses carefully selected three-part words.
